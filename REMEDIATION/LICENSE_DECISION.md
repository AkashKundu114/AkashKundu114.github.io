## 2. Decision

The portfolio repository itself is released under the **MIT License**.

### Rationale:
1. **Recruiter & Community Accessibility:** The MIT License allows recruiters, engineers, and researchers to review, fork, inspect, and benchmark the portfolio code with zero legal friction.
2. **Permissive & Standard:** Widely recognized across top-tier engineering organizations (Google, Meta, Microsoft).
3. **Attribution Protected:** Retains copyright notice (`Copyright (c) 2026 Akash Kundu`) while disclaiming liability for third-party forks.
